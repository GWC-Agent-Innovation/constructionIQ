# Privacy and room relationships

Start with a public zone near the requested entrance and a quieter bedroom
zone. Prefer kitchen next to dining. Use compact shared access for bedrooms,
without routing visitors through a bedroom to reach another required space.

Explicit required_adjacencies take priority over general preferences. Sharing
a wall and having a door connection are different: do not assume adjacency
alone guarantees access. Common bathrooms need public access; attached bathrooms
need private direct access only from their assigned bedroom. Never change an
explicit assignment to imitate a reference.

Compare alternatives by useful room proportions, compact routes and privacy,
not only by visual symmetry. Keep all requested rooms and assignments intact.
Explain design intentions honestly; do not claim validated ventilation,
furniture clearance, construction readiness or code compliance.
