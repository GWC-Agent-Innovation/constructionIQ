# Kitchen and dining relationship

ConstructIQ requirement-handling rule: an explicit kitchen-next-to-dining
request is a shared-wall requirement, not merely a style adjective.
Honor HouseSpec.required_adjacencies in every candidate. Consider the latest
request as well; do not claim it is met simply because it appears in notes.

Sharing a wall, having a door, and having a usable walking route are different.
Keep sufficient shared span for the existing door planner (currently 3.6 ft).
A bathroom column between kitchen and dining breaks adjacency even if both
open to the hallway. Rearrange columns or rotate a group instead of relaxing
the request. If it cannot be achieved, return an infeasible proposal for repair.
