# Private ensuite assignment

Each recorded attached_bathroom_bedrooms entry is a bedroom number with its
own private bathroom. Preserve these assignments while rearranging partitions.
Place the bathroom on a wall shared with its bedroom and reserve enough shared
span for the door planner. Check that the bedroom still has useful furniture
space after the suite is split.

Try end-bath and side-bath suite groupings; pick the one that limits wasted
circulation without making the bedroom a narrow strip. An ensuite need not
span the entire width of a large bedroom. If fitting a compact bathroom would
leave a huge hallway alcove, regroup the adjacent rooms.
This is ConstructIQ planning guidance, not a plumbing/structural approval.
