# Review a proposed home honestly

ConstructIQ quality checks, separate from legal/structural review:
- Validate room counts, assignments, shared walls, entrance and door paths.
- Compute all hallway/alcove area divided by footprint area. Flag above 15%.
- For balanced-bedroom briefs, compare largest/smallest bedroom-only areas;
  flag above 1.25 unless a master bedroom was requested.
- Check kitchen/dining adjacency from geometry, not planning_notes.
- Review plausible furniture footprints, route positions and exterior access.
  Mark unmodeled dimensions/clearances as unverified, never as passed.

These thresholds are application heuristics. A score is not architect approval.
When repairing, report the failed measurement and change the relevant grouping.
Keep unrelated explicit requirements. Do not drop a room to improve the score.
