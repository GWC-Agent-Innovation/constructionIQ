# Daylight and air are design inputs

BEE's residential envelope guidance treats heat gain, daylight and ventilation
as separate performance considerations. Openable window area differs from
the visible glazing area; orientation and climate matter.[^bee]
This source landing page lists multiple ENS editions; no climate-specific
numeric compliance limit is asserted here.

ConstructIQ heuristic: try to place occupied rooms along available external
edges and identify which spaces would otherwise be internal. Do not assume
a window touching the plot boundary is legally possible: neighbors, setbacks
and site conditions are unknown. Do not promise cross-ventilation just because
two windows were rendered. No airflow, daylight or overheating simulation is
performed. Entrance direction alone does not establish the best solar design.

[^bee]: Bureau of Energy Efficiency, residential Eco Niwas Samhita resources.
