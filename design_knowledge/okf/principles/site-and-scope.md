# Site and building footprint

This bundle improves concept planning; no document has architect sign-off.
Before construction, establish site survey, road/access side, municipality,
setbacks, permitted coverage, soil and structural system. Plot size alone is
not the legal buildable footprint. BIS describes NBC as a model code spanning
development control, structure, fire safety and services.[^bis]

Application instruction: the current compiler tiles the supplied rectangle.
Do not invent setbacks or assert that the whole plot can legally be built on.
Treat its rectangle as a concept envelope; flag missing site information.
Coordinates describe room zones, not finished clear space after wall thickness.
Accessibility, drainage, structural spans and construction approval are unverified.
Keep all recorded rooms, entrance direction and attached-bath assignments.
Do not invent a courtyard or a new room to absorb surplus area.

[^bis]: BIS, National Building Code overview.
