# Compact circulation

ConstructIQ heuristic: aim for 5-10% dedicated hallway area when a hall is
needed. Zero is acceptable if public rooms provide access. The AI compiler's
15% cap and 5-6 ft short-side rule are software constraints, not universal
building regulations. Include EVERY bathroom/suite alcove in the area total.

Trace entrance to living, kitchen, each bedroom and common bathroom. Guests
must not cross a bedroom or ensuite to reach another destination. Keep routes
outside the primary seating and cooking positions where possible.
A hallway larger than living is a reason to regroup partitions.
Do not relabel a leftover corridor as living to improve the metric.
If a compact bathroom leaves a large alcove, change the suite orientation,
public-band depth or entire tree. Do not increase bathroom area past its cap.
