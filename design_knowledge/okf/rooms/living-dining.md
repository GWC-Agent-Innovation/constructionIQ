# Living and dining with usable circulation

ConstructIQ heuristic: draw an imagined furniture footprint before allocating
room area. Keep a usable sofa/TV zone and a dining-table zone; allow a route
around them instead of treating all open floor area as circulation.
Living should receive the entrance naturally while bedroom doors remain away
from the main visitor path. A wide public opening is not a furniture-fit test.

For dining, NKBA's cited seating guidance distinguishes 32 in behind a seated
diner with no through traffic, 36 in to edge past, and 44 in to walk past.[^nkba]
Measure from the table edge; these examples are not room dimensions or a
universal accessibility standard. Use the table/chair sizes requested by the
user. If no separate dining room is requested, retain a plausible dining zone
in living without creating a dining catalog leaf.

[^nkba]: NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines, seating traffic clearance guideline 8.
