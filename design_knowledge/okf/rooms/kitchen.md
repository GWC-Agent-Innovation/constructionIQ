# Kitchen work space

NKBA's published guidance recommends 42 in clear work aisles for one cook,
48 in for multiple cooks, and 36 in for a walkway. Appliance/entry doors
should not obstruct each other; major through-traffic should avoid the
cooking work area.[^nkba] These are the cited edition's recommendations,
not an assertion of local code compliance.

ConstructIQ application: prefer kitchen next to dining; reserve a coherent
counter wall and appliance positions. As an illustration, assumed 2 ft counters
on both sides plus a 3.5 ft work aisle need 7.5 ft CLEAR internal width, before
wall construction. A narrow room zone cannot be called a verified galley.
Keep a public access route; kitchen must not be the bedroom corridor.
The current renderer's furniture is illustrative: clearance and appliance
door checks have NOT been performed.

[^nkba]: NKBA Kitchen Planning Guidelines, guidelines 2, 5, 6 and 7.
