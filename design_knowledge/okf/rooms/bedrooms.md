# Bedrooms that can hold furniture

External comparison only: England's NDSS gives 7.5 m2 for a single bedroom,
11.5 m2 for a double/twin, and width criteria of 2.15 m for a single and
2.75 m for at least one double (2.55 m for other doubles). These are English
planning standards, not Indian rules or this app's acceptance thresholds.[^ndss]

ConstructIQ heuristic: for an unspecified family bedroom, try roughly
10 x 12 ft or 11 x 12 ft zones before allocating surplus to hallways.
These starting points do not prove furniture fit. Imagine the actual bed,
wardrobe depth, entry swing and a route to both storage and bathroom.
An equal-bedroom request should produce comparable bedroom-only areas;
do not count the ensuite as bedroom space. A largest/smallest ratio above
1.25 is a QA warning unless a master suite was explicitly requested.
Prefer useful widths and an exterior edge. Never silently reduce room count.

[^ndss]: England, nationally described space standard, bedroom criteria.
