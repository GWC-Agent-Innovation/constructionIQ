# Parking requires a vehicle and access route

ConstructIQ heuristic: choose a bay around the actual vehicle footprint plus
door-opening, pedestrian and maneuvering space. Confirm road access and gate
position before claiming a car can enter. A parking rectangle on an external
edge alone does not establish a feasible turning path.

Keep parking from cutting off the home's entrance or the only room windows.
Respect the current parking catalog minimum and user-configured maximum
(default 250 sq ft). Never enlarge parking merely to fill unused plot area.
If vehicle dimensions or access geometry are unknown, treat the bay as
conceptual. Turning radius, slopes, drainage and street permissions are not
modeled. This document contains application heuristics, not prescribed bay
dimensions or local parking regulations.
