# Bathrooms and wet areas

ConstructIQ heuristic: group bathrooms and kitchen/service areas so a future
plumbing design can use short service routes where practical. This is a design
intention, not a calculated pipe or drainage system.
Provide public access to common bathrooms. Give an ensuite access only from
its assigned bedroom; never use it as a passage to another room.

Sketch toilet, basin and shower footprints plus door swing and standing space.
A 5 x 8 ft room zone is only an illustrative starting point, not proof that
any fixture configuration fits. Apply the actual catalog minimum and the
configured bathroom area/bedroom-ratio caps. Do not use an enormous bathroom
or hallway alcove to complete a rectangular suite.
Favor an external wall or an identified ventilation solution. The current
model cannot certify exhaust, waterproofing, drainage slope or fixture clearances.
